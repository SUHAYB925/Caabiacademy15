FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Su'aalaha Inta Badan La Is Weydiiyo.

Halkan ka hel jawaabaha su'aalaha caanka ah ee ku saabsan waxbarashada iyo adeegyada Caabi Academy.

Sideen iskaga diiwaangeliyaa Caabi Academy?

Booqo bogga Sign Up, buuxi xogta lagaa rabo, kadibna raac tilmaamaha ka muuqda website-ka.

Sideen u arki karaa koorsooyinka la heli karo?

Booqo bogga Courses si aad u aragto koorsooyinka, faahfaahintooda iyo qiimahooda.

Maxaan sameeyaa haddii aan dhibaato farsamo qabo?

Foomka xiriirka ka dooro Technical Support, kuna sharax dhibaatada ku haysata si faahfaahsan.

Sideen u soo jeedin karaa fikrad ama wada-shaqayn?

Isticmaal foomka kore, dooro Feedback ama Partnership, kadibna noo sharax fikraddaada.
